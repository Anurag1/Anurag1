# RevenueGraph AI

RevenueGraph AI is a research-to-revenue experimentation layer for the Anurag1 ecosystem.

It connects opportunity discovery, evidence and research, content generation, authorized social publishing, attribution, conversion experiments, and revenue measurement.

## MVP

The first experiment is the AI Discovery Benchmark:
1. identify an evidence-backed AI insight;
2. publish platform-specific content;
3. send traffic to a benchmark/landing page;
4. attribute clicks and conversions;
5. record revenue;
6. feed results back into the opportunity graph.

## Principle

Do not optimize for posting volume or followers. Optimize for measurable learning and attributable economic outcomes.

No platform credentials are stored in the repository. Production publishing must use the platform's permitted APIs and authentication flows.
