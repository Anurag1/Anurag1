# Experiment 001 — AI Discovery Benchmark

## Hypothesis

Evidence-backed content about a reproducible AI discovery benchmark can attract qualified technical users to the benchmark and create a measurable path to paid technical work or software.

## Artifact

A benchmark containing 13 deliberately cross-domain problems comparing:
- conventional LLM baseline
- graph/contradiction-based discovery approach

## Content sequence

1. Publish one concrete benchmark observation.
2. Explain the test rather than claiming a breakthrough.
3. Link to the reproducible artifact.
4. Track source-specific clicks.
5. Track downloads or signups.
6. Record attributable commercial conversion.

## Metrics

Primary:
- qualified leads
- attributable revenue

Secondary:
- click-through rate
- benchmark downloads
- repeat visits
- qualified replies

## Stop conditions

Stop or redesign the experiment if:
- evidence cannot be reproduced;
- traffic is non-qualified;
- conversion remains negligible after a predefined sample;
- content requires unsupported claims to generate attention.

## Decision rule

The experiment produces a learning result, not a guaranteed business outcome.
